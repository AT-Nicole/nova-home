Why source directly from the factory

We are not a trading company. PCBs are produced in-house by our group’s electronics plant, and five production lines handle assembly with a four-step QC system on every batch. When you buy from WECHGOOD, the people you talk to sit in the same building where your products are made.

OEM and ODM options for induction cookers

Start with brand label on our standard 2100W portable models (MOQ 500 pcs), move to custom OEM with your own color, panel and packaging, or go full ODM with industrial design, PCB and firmware developed by our engineers.

What to check before ordering induction cookers

Voltage for your market (220-240V standard, custom available), certifications required by your local authority, packaging and carton size for your shipping route, and MOQ alignment. Send us your market requirements and we will confirm all four within 12 working hours.

Frequently asked questions

What is your MOQ for induction cookers?

Our standard MOQ is 500 pcs per model. First trial orders can be discussed - contact our export team with your quantity.

Can you put our brand on induction cookers?

Yes. OEM logo on product, carton and manual, custom color, voltage, plug and packaging. See our OEM/ODM page for the three service levels.

What certifications do your induction cookers have?

CE, RoHS, ErP, UKCA, LVD and EMC. Certificate PDFs are available for download on our FAQ page.

What is your lead time?

35-40 days after deposit confirmation for standard models.
