Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions importers ask us most - from MOQ to lead time, samples and OEM.

What is your MOQ?

500 pcs per model - for all models, always. Sample orders are welcome before bulk commitment.

Which certifications do your products carry?

Our WQ-80 series (8 models) carries the full EU CE suite - RoHS (2011/65/EU), EMC (2014/30/EU), LVD (2014/35/EU, EN 60335-2-9) - plus UKCA for the UK and ErP standby power testing. Certificate copies and test reports are available on request for covered models.

Can you customize voltage and plug for my market?

Yes. We build 220-240V / 110V variants with EU, UK, US or AU plugs to match your destination market. Several models feature universal voltage (AC220-240V) as standard. Custom certification support (e.g. SNI for Indonesia) can be arranged.

What is the lead time?

35-45 days after deposit received, confirmed with your quotation. 5 production lines and 300,000 pcs monthly capacity let us schedule standard orders and OEM projects in parallel.

Can I order samples first?

Yes - we support trial orders and sample requests at any time. Sample charge per model is confirmed with your quotation, so you can test quality before the bulk order.

How does the OEM/ODM process work?

Simple flow: (1) share your idea, drawing or reference photo; (2) we confirm 2D/3D drawings and spec sheet; (3) sample for your approval; (4) pilot run; (5) mass production with 4-step QC. Our sales engineers reply within 12 working hours at every stage.

Which markets do you export to?

40+ countries, including the EU, North America, the Middle East, Southeast Asia and India. Site and documentation available in English, Arabic, Spanish and Thai.

What payment terms do you accept?

Export terms are quoted FOB / CFR / CIF per your requirement; payment terms are confirmed per order (T/T common).

What about warranty and after-sales?

Every batch passes our 4-step QC before packing. Warranty and after-sales terms are specified in the sales contract; factory defects are covered per the agreed policy.

Where can I download your catalog and certificates?

From the Downloads section below - 2025 Company Profile & Catalog, plus certificate PDFs. Or request the latest version via the inquiry form (we reply within 12 working hours).
