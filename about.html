About Us

A Chinese home appliance factory with 15 years of export experience, serving importers and distributors worldwide.

Foshan, Guangdong, China

From workshop to world market

We started in 2010 as a small appliance workshop in Guangdong, China, assembling induction cookers for the domestic market. Year by year we invested in tooling, testing equipment and international standards until our products began shipping to Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Europe and the Americas.

Today our factory covers 12,000 square meters with five production lines and 200+ workers. We still do the same thing we did on day one: build reliable appliances, test them properly, and ship them on time.

Most of our business comes from repeat orders and referrals. That is the measure we care about most.
