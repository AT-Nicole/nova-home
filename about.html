About Us

Rare among Chinese manufacturers, we stand out as a leader in the induction cooker and electric ceramic hob industry—with an annual sales volume of 5 million units.

Foshan, Guangdong Province, China

From workshop to world market

Founded in November 2015 as the home appliance arm of the Xinke group (established in 2008 as a PCBA manufacturer), we started assembling induction cookers in Foshan and invested year by year in tooling, testing equipment and international standards, until our products began shipping to Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Europe and the Americas.

Today our factory covers more than 18,000 square meters with five production lines and 300+ workers. We still do the same thing we did on day one: build reliable appliances, test them properly, and ship them on time.

Most of our business comes from repeat orders and referrals. That is the measure we care about most.

Rare among Chinese manufacturers, we stand out as a leader in the induction cooker and electric ceramic hob industry—with an annual sales volume of 5 million units. As the original OEM/ODM source factory for top-tier brands including Joyoung, Xiaomi, Haier, Royalstar and Airmate, we’ve established a proven track record of excellence. While we previously focused on providing OEM services for these leading brands, we are now expanding into the global market.

Our core advantages are as follows:

  • In-house core component production (including PCBA and heating coil plate) and integrated manufacturing capabilities, enabling us to offer highly competitive pricing and reliable quality
  • Strong technical expertise, backed by over 10 years of deep cooperation with top-tier brands
  • Advanced product design and innovation capabilities, tailored to global market demands

OEM / ODM capabilities

Your brand, your specs, your packaging. We handle the rest.

How we work with your brand - three ways

From private label on proven models to full product development, one factory covers it all.

01

Tier 01 - Brand Label

Your logo on our proven models: logo printing, branded color box, manual and packaging in your market language - on the same lines that build for Joyoung, Haier and Xiaomi ecosystem partners.

MOQ: 500 pcs per model

Best for: brands validating a category, importers building a first private-label container

02

Tier 02 - Custom OEM

Deeper customization on mature platforms: market-specific voltage and plug, control panel language, housing color, accessory kit and packaging structure.

MOQ: 500 pcs per model

Best for: distributors with fixed market specifications

03

Tier 03 - Full ODM

Your product, our factory: from brief to container - industrial design, tooling, electronics and firmware, certification support. SKD / CKD export service available.

MOQ: quoted per project

Best for: brands entering the category with their own design

Tooling & certificates

Standard-platform tooling stays with WECHGOOD; custom tooling for Tier 03 projects is discussed per project. Certificate copies and test reports are available for covered models.
